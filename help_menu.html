page auto-resizes to fit screen size (turn off "Desktop Mode" on mobile). press F11 on desktop for full screen, or add to home screen on mobile.

entries marked [FLASHING] contain photosensitive animations.

Species

searchable by keyword or scrollable.

Form

standard dropdown without search features.

Personality

searchable by keyword or scrollable. "Fly" auras replace their standard versions for flying/floating dragons in-game. entries with internal resource names in parentheses are unused in-game.

Chroma

emulates Chroma shaders in Dragon Village 3. each species in DV3 is limited to one Chroma channel in-game, but this tool freely allows for the selection of any of the five Chroma channels used across species.

Background/Floor

selection retains brightness and animation state when changed

Animation

dragon animations; "None" falls back to setup pose, which may differ from in-game sprite. selected animation will persist if possible when changing species. if unavailable, it will default back to “Idle”.

Spotlight

spotlight that appears over the top 20 rankers in a server. "Burst" plays the animation that appears when a ranker taps their own dragon in the Square.

Genetic Information

skin settings for body parts. can only be used with Dragon Village Nest breeding partners (Dracon, Jade, Nira, Luti).

Sharelink

copies a sharelink of the current dragon, personality, and cave decor to the clipboard.
