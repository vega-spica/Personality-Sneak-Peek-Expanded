entries with animations that may be harmful to users with photosensitivity are labelled with "FLASHING".

Species

can be searched by keyword or scrolled through.

Form

a standard dropdown without search features.

Personality

can be searched by keyword or scrolled through. entries labelled "Fly" replace the normal versions in-game if a model is designated as flying (or floating) above the ground. entries with internal resource names in parentheses are dummied out and inaccessible in-game.

Chroma

applies shaders that emulate those used for Chromas in Dragon Village 3. each species in DV3 is limited to one Chroma channel in-game, but this tool freely allows for the selection of any of the five channels available in DV3's Chroma Check developer menu.

Background/Floor

animation state and brightness will persist even if the selection is changed.

Animation

refers to dragon animations. the "None" animation falls back to the setup pose, which may not necessarily match the in-game sprite for a dragon. the selected animation will normally persist even when the species is changed. however, certain animations are only available when a DV3 model is selected. DV3-exclusive animations will default back to “Idle” when the species is next changed.

Spotlight

the spotlight that appears over the top 20 rankers in a server. the Burst animation is the halo burst that appears when a ranker taps their own dragon in the Square.

Sharelink

copies a sharelink to the clipboard. this exports the current dragon as it is, with its personality and cave decorations.
