welcome to <a href="https://vega-spica.github.io/Personality-Sneak-Peek-Expanded/">Dragon Village Collection Plus: APPEND</a>. as of August of 2026, after two and a half years of operation under the name of "Dragon Village Collection: Personality Sneak Peek (Expanded)", this site has finally been overhauled and repurposed as an archival tool. i would like to extend my deepest thanks to both highbrow staff and users worldwide for their support and feedback over the years.

in this day and age, data preservation has become ever more important, especially in the case of live-service games or the discontinuation of media that is not distributed in physical form. while Dragon Village Collection will continue to be the main focus of this tool, additional elements will occasionally be appended to showcase alternative models and renders that are featured in other Dragon Village media.

this is an unofficial tool, and i don't receive any money for making it. but if you’d like to see more archival like this, consider contributing to the <a href="https://dragon-village-collection.fandom.com/wiki/">DVC Fandom Wiki</a>.
